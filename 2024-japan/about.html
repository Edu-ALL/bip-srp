ABOUT US

EduALL is an integrative uni-prep and mentoring for middle & high school students established in 2016. We are here to foster a growth mindset, develop academic & life skills, and facilitate access to high-quality education globally and real-world experiences, all while having fun in the process. At the end of the day, we hope to empower students to become independent thinkers and achieve their own definition of success.
