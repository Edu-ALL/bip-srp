Program Overview

EduALL Global Immersion Program is a career exploration program in Japan for students who are interested in science and technology fields. We provide a real-life implementation in Aerospace Engineering, Sustainable/Green Technology, Genetic Engineering, and Pharmaceutical Sciences topics through workshops, company and university visits, and projects with guidance from coaches and experts in respective fields. 

You will end the program with tangible outcomes that could be the start of your projects in the future. Through this program, we want you to broaden your horizons and take action, connect with like-minded students, and confirm the career outlook and university major that you want to pursue.

Students need direct exposure to multiple streams of a career as they explore their interests and passions. By combining fun excursions, multidisciplinary workshops, and insightful company visits, students will discover their potential further, get exposed to various in-demand areas, and be equipped with the steps and pathways to a successful career. With personal engagement towards learning, they will grow inherent motivation to learn and develop both technically, mentally, and socially.

This year, the Global Immersion Program will cover four topics: Aerospace Engineering, Sustainable/Green Technology, Genetic Engineering, and Pharmaceutical Sciences.

The ideal candidate for this program is a middle or high school student from Grade 9 to 12 who is driven, and considering studying in Japan and pursuing job opportunities there.

Any previous knowledge or experience in Science, Technology, Engineering, or Math is not required.

Global Immersion Program is a 10-day program that runs from 15 - 24 December 2024.
The application is open from May to the end of October 2024 for students who require a visa and the second week of November for students who do not require visa (Indonesian e-passport holder) on a first-come-first-serve basis.

Fee and Scholarship

This program costs 3,450 USD.

The fee includes the cost for accommodation + breakfast, travel insurance, ground transport (with a variety of coaches and public transport), meals (18x), all program entrance fees, and a program certificate. This fee excludes visa fee (if needed), flight tickets, souvenirs and personal shopping, and extra meals on free days.

The program fee does not include the flight ticket, but we can help with the flight arrangement along with other participants.

Unfortunately, we are not opening scholarship opportunities for this program. However, you can earn a special fee with our offers. Fill in the form and ask our team for available offers!
